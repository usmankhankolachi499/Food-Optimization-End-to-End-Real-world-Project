How MOON Light works

One platform from menu to doorstep

MOON Light is a Food Delivery Optimization System built with Flask. It combines ordering, live GPS tracking, smart partner assignment, route optimisation and operational analytics in a single role-based web application.

Three roles Live tracking Optimised routes Reports
The workflow

How an order moves through the system

Every step is visible to the right people through role-based dashboards.

1

Browse & build a cart

Customers discover restaurants by cuisine, city, rating or opening hours, then add dishes from a single restaurant menu.

2

Checkout with a location

The delivery address and map point are captured at checkout. The delivery fee is calculated from the real distance to the restaurant.

3

Smart assignment

The system ranks available partners by proximity, current workload, delivery radius and — when available — an ML scoring hook.

4

Preparation & pickup

The partner accepts the job, marks the food as prepared, then starts the delivery. Status changes notify the customer automatically.

5

Live tracking & route optimisation

Location points stream to the tracking API. The customer sees the partner on a map while batched orders are sequenced pickup-before-drop.

6

Feedback & analytics

Delivered orders can be rated. Administrators review delivery times, peak hours, partner performance, revenue and CSV reports.

  • Order history, cancellation and star ratings

Administrators

Under the hood

Transparent, configurable optimisation

Travel time uses the configured average speed and traffic factor. Partner ranking weighs distance, workload and ratings. Preparation buffers and delivery radius are tuned from the admin settings screen. If a separate ML predictor module is installed, it is used automatically for ETAs and partner scoring — otherwise deterministic fallbacks keep the app fully functional.
